VITAGRAPH VIDEO STYLE BIBLE
The visual law for every clip. Already pasted, in full, inside every prompt file.
Source of truth: VitaGraph-App-v3.html, design/reference/app-v3-source.html, design/reference/screens/*.png, site design/src/theme/tokens.css.
==========================================================================================

1. WHAT THE VIDEO MUST FEEL LIKE
--------------------------------
A live, precise, software-drawn interface animation in the strict Swiss International (Modernist) style: flat, architectural, quiet, exact. It must look as if VitaGraph's own canvas code drew it in real time inside the app. Think of a perfectly engineered instrument panel designed by a Modernist graphic designer: Josef Muller-Brockmann grids, Dieter Rams restraint, Massimo Vignelli discipline. Never cinematic, never sci-fi, never "AI", never stock footage.


2. CANVAS AND FORMAT
--------------------
- Square 1:1 frame. Reference size 1600 x 1600 px (1% of the frame = 16 px). Any size of at least 1080 x 1080 is acceptable.
- 24 frames per second. Silent (no audio track needed).
- Coordinates in every prompt: x from 0% (left edge) to 100% (right edge), y from 0% (top edge) to 100% (bottom edge).
- SAFE AREA: every meaningful object stays inside x 10%-90% and y 10%-90%. The outer ring (0-10% on every side) is plain field, because the app crops the frame differently on different screens.
- RESERVED CORNER: x 74%-100%, y 86%-100% stays completely empty in every frame (the app draws its own "Move · Scroll" tag there).
- If a tool can only make 16:9: compose everything inside the central square of the 16:9 frame and keep the left and right sides as plain field.


3. BACKGROUND
-------------
- The whole frame is ONE perfectly flat, perfectly uniform field of warm light grey #EAE9E9 (the app's surface colour), identical in every pixel, edge to edge, in every frame.
- No vignette, no gradient, no light falloff, no texture, no paper grain, no noise, no dust, no pattern.
- Optional and only if the generator can keep it perfectly still and perfectly straight: a square grid of 2 px lines in ink #201E1D at 6% opacity (it reads as a very faint grey, about #DCDBDA), one line every 6% of the frame width, locked to the frame, never moving, never bending. If the grid wobbles, swims, shimmers or bends in any frame, DROP THE GRID. A flat field is always better than a moving grid.


4. COLOUR PALETTE (the only colours that may appear)
-----------------------------------------------------
Neutrals (warm greys, slightly red-brown, never blue):
  #EAE9E9  field / surface         warm light grey: the background
  #F3F2F2  page ground             a hair lighter than the field
  #F8F4F4  paper                   the lightest warm white-grey: documents, cards, bars' backgrounds
  #D7D3D3  light grey              tracks, tags, empty states
  #BBB8B7  faint ink               unread text bars (ink at 28% on paper)
  #BAB6B6  mid grey                grey dots, top faces of grey bars
  #999796  divider grey            the 2 px outlines of cards and pages (ink at 40%)
  #605D5D  dark grey               secondary bars, measurement dots, front faces of grey bars
  #2D2B2B  darkest grey            side faces of grey bars
  #201E1D  ink                     near-black warm ink: read text, marks, outlines that matter
The one accent (vivid vermilion red, pure, never orange, never pink, never magenta):
  #EC3013  red                     the active thing: scan bars, cut marks, progress, focus, the user's own scope
  #AE1800  deep red                small red marks, citation chips, side faces of red bars
  #FF9783  light red               top faces of red bars only
  #FFC4B8  pale red                highlighted passage, intensified
  #FFE0D9  paler red               highlighted passage, reference bands
  #FFF2EF  palest red              refusal card fill only
Rules:
- No other hue exists: no blue, cyan, teal, green, yellow, gold, orange, purple, violet, pink neon, rainbow.
- Red is rare and meaningful. At any moment it marks only the single process that is happening now. Red never covers more than about 8% of the frame.
- No colour gradients anywhere. Every shape is one flat colour (opacity changes over time are allowed).


5. GEOMETRY
-----------
- Zero corner radius on everything. Every rectangle has razor-sharp 90-degree corners. No rounded rectangles, no pills, no soft corners.
- True circles are allowed only where a prompt says "dot", "circle", "ring" or "pulse ring".
- Lines are perfectly straight (except the gentle curved graph edges in the graph clips). Line weights at 1600 px: hairline 2 px, rule 4 px, heavy rule 6 px.
- Documents and cards: flat paper #F8F4F4 rectangles with a 2 px divider-grey #999796 outline. Never a drop shadow, never a 3D page curl, never a fold.
- Text is NEVER drawn. Every place where text would be is a solid horizontal bar with square ends ("greeked" text): headings = short thick ink bars, body lines = thinner bars, unread text = faint ink #BBB8B7, read text = ink #201E1D.
- Composition: aligned to an invisible grid, flush left, generous empty space, strong horizontal and vertical alignment, nothing centred for decoration.


6. LIGHT AND RENDERING
----------------------
- No lighting at all. The image is flat like a vector drawing on a screen: no shading, no highlights, no ambient occlusion, no reflections, no gloss, no bevel, no emboss, no glass, no translucency effects (plain opacity is fine).
- No shadows of any kind (not even soft drop shadows).
- No glow, no bloom, no neon, no light rays, no lens flare, no light leaks, no bokeh.
- No blur of any kind: no depth of field, no motion blur, no gaussian blur, no defocus. Every edge in every frame is crisp, like a vector shape rendered at infinite shutter speed.
- No film grain, no noise, no chromatic aberration, no scanlines, no CRT effect, no compression look.


7. CAMERA
---------
- Locked-off, tripod-static, orthographic, perfectly straight-on view. The frame itself never moves.
- No zoom, no pan, no tilt, no dolly, no rotation, no camera shake, no handheld drift, no parallax.
- One continuous take. No cuts, no transitions, no wipes, no flashes, no fades to black.
- The only "3D" allowed is drawn: the perspective floor grid and the rotating node cloud in the graph clips (the model rotates, never the camera), and the true isometric bars in the timeline clip.


8. MOTION
---------
- Calm, precise, mechanical, confident: like a premium product interface, not like an advert.
- Objects move only in straight lines, mostly purely horizontal or purely vertical.
- Easing: ease-out cubic (quick start, long soft settle). No bounce, no overshoot, no elastic wobble, no spring jiggle, no squash and stretch.
- Appearing objects: fade from 0 to 100% opacity over 0.3 to 0.5 s, optionally while sliding 1% to 3% into place. Lines may "draw on" from one end. Bars may "grow" from their left end.
- Allowed scaling: only where a prompt says so, smoothly over at least 0.8 s.
- No morphing blobs, no liquid, no smoke, no particles, no sparkles, no confetti, no shimmer, no flicker, no strobing, no pulsing glow. (The single allowed "pulse" is the app's own focus ring: one thin red circle that expands and fades once.)
- Motion is steady and slow enough that frames taken every 1/3 s still look continuous.
- Every single frame must look like a finished, sharp, still UI screenshot. The user stops on any frame.
- Chain clips: the first 0.5 s and the last 0.5 s are perfectly still holds.


9. STRICTLY FORBIDDEN CONTENT
-----------------------------
- Any text, letter, digit, number, symbol, punctuation, logo, watermark, signature, caption, subtitle, label, icon font, emoji.
- Any user-interface chrome: mouse cursor, pointer, hand, finger, window frame, browser bar, menu, button with an icon, scroll bar.
- People, faces, hands, bodies, silhouettes, doctors, patients.
- Medical cliches: DNA helix, brain, heart, heartbeat line, stethoscope, pills, capsules, syringe, red cross, hospital, X-ray, molecules, cells.
- Tech cliches: holograms, glowing circuits, binary code, matrix rain, HUD overlays, sci-fi interfaces, neon wireframes, glowing spheres, robots, futuristic city, space, stars, nebula.
- Photorealism, 3D renders, clay, plastic, glass, metal, cartoon, anime, sketch, watercolour, hand-drawn lines, paper texture.
(Exception: clip 13 shows one black-and-white photograph of a printed sheet inside a flat frame, as the Image to Text page does.)


10. THE STYLE LOCK PARAGRAPH (pasted at the start of every master prompt)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
A pristine flat 2D motion-graphics animation that looks exactly like a live vector user interface drawn by software, in strict Swiss International (Modernist) graphic style. Square 1:1 frame, 24 fps, silent. The entire frame is one perfectly flat, perfectly uniform field of warm light grey #EAE9E9, identical in every pixel, edge to edge, no vignette, no texture, no noise, no gradient. All shapes are flat single-colour geometry with razor-sharp straight edges and perfectly square 90-degree corners: zero corner radius anywhere; only dots and rings are round. The only colours are warm greys (paper #F8F4F4, light grey #D7D3D3, faint ink #BBB8B7, divider grey #999796, dark grey #605D5D), near-black warm ink #201E1D, and one vivid vermilion red #EC3013 (deep red #AE1800, pale red #FFE0D9 only where stated); no other hue exists. Red marks only the single thing that is active right now. Absolutely no text of any kind: no letters, no numbers, no symbols, no logos, no watermark; every place where writing would be is drawn as solid horizontal bars with square ends. No lighting, no shading, no shadows, no glow, no bloom, no reflections, no blur, no depth of field, no motion blur, no film grain. Locked-off static orthographic camera, straight-on, no zoom, no pan, no cuts, one continuous take. Motion is calm, precise and mechanical, along straight horizontal or vertical lines, ease-out cubic, no bounce, no overshoot, no particles. Every frame must look like a finished, razor-sharp UI screenshot. Keep all objects inside the central area between 10% and 90% of the frame on both axes, and keep the bottom-right corner region empty.
