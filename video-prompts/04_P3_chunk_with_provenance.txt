This clip shows that meaning without words: the whole report's text, now complete, becomes one continuous strip; red cut marks divide it into equal passages; each passage drops into its own card; every card visibly remembers which page it came from and exactly where in that page it sits (its character span), and finally each card shows how many entities it contains.


B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
VitaGraph splits the extracted text into sentence-aware chunks (target about 200 characters, user-adjustable from 120 to 600 in Settings). Every chunk keeps its provenance: report id, page number, and the exact start and end character offsets inside that page's text. These offsets are what later let the app highlight the exact cited characters. The graph extractor then counts the entities (tests, values, dates) in each chunk. The reference demo has 24 chunks over five pages (pages give about 6, 5, 5, 6 and 2 chunks); those proportions are used below; no number is drawn.
