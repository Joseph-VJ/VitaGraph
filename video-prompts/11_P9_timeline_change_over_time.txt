A. WHAT THIS CLIP IS FOR
------------------------
VitaGraph is longitudinal: it reads many reports over the years and shows how one value changes. This clip builds the Timeline page's signature chart: a true isometric 3D bar chart with a floor, a back wall, a left wall, a pale red reference band across both walls, and one box per report rising from the floor; older reports in grey, the latest report in vivid red. Use it in a demo film or as an alternative frame set.


B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
For one biomarker (for example hemoglobin in g/dL), the Timeline page collects the value from every report that really contains it (only points the report confirms), orders them by the printed report date, takes the reference range printed on the report, and draws the reference's isometric chart: P(x, y, z) = (ox + (x - y) * 0.866, oy + (x + y) * 0.5 - z), chart length 260, depth 70, height 120 units, axis maximum 17 in the reference demo, band from 13 to 17, bars 34 x 34 units placed along time (Jan 2024 at the start, Aug 2024 about a quarter along, Feb 2026 at the end), heights 13.1, 13.2 and 14.0. Grey bars: top #BAB6B6, front #605D5D, side #2D2B2B; latest bar: top #FF9783, front #EC3013, side #AE1800; every face outlined by a thin ink line. Values and dates are HTML labels on the page; in this video they are short bars, never digits.
