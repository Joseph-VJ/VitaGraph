B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
VitaGraph upserts every chunk vector into a local ChromaDB store with the persona's user_id as metadata. Every later search runs with the filter where user_id = this persona, so another persona's reports can never be retrieved (privacy by construction; the AI Agent's tools are also locked to the persona by the backend, never by a tool argument). Each upload writes audit entries (upload accepted with its SHA-256 hash, chunks indexed, embeddings upserted). A question later retrieves the top 5 most similar passages. The picture shows exactly this; no ids, hashes or numbers are drawn.
