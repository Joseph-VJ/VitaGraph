A 6-second silent flat 2D vector motion-graphics clip in strict Swiss International (Modernist) style, 16:9 landscape (1920 x 1080), 24 fps, one continuous locked-off take. It will be shown very softly behind a web page and scrubbed by the scroll wheel, so it must work as a sequence of beautiful, readable stills. The entire frame is one perfectly flat, uniform PURE WHITE field #FFFFFF, identical in every pixel, no vignette, no gradient, no texture, no noise. Everything drawn is clean line work and small flat marks with perfectly square 90-degree corners (zero corner radius; only dots, rings and the lens are round). Strokes are 2 to 4 px (never thicker than 6 px). Colours allowed: ink #201E1D, darkest grey #2D2B2B, dark grey #605D5D, divider grey #999796, mid grey #BAB6B6, light grey #D7D3D3, paper #F8F4F4, and ONE vivid vermilion red #EC3013 (pale red #FFC4B8 only for highlights); no other hue exists; red covers less than 4% of the frame and marks only what is active. No lighting, no shading, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no dark backgrounds, no neon.
SOFTNESS: the hero object and the words being read stay sharp and legible. Everything secondary (the other papers of the batch, background cards, extra dots, small details) is slightly soft, a light blur of about 1 to 2 px at 1920 px. Fast-moving pieces carry a short light motion blur along their direction of travel (about 3 to 6 px). Never heavy blur, never blur on the hero text.
PACE: fast and busy, like a machine working through a whole batch of papers, not a single page: first the sharp hero action, then immediately a rapid burst of the same action over many soft copies. Many elements move at once, ease-out cubic, straight paths, no bounce, no particles. The first and last frame are each a complete, balanced composition.
COMPOSITION: weighted to the RIGHT; the left 38% of the frame stays almost empty (a page heading will sit there); at least half of the frame stays empty white; everything inside x 10%-90% and y 6%-94%.
