A 6-second silent flat 2D vector motion-graphics clip in strict Swiss International (Modernist) style, 16:9 landscape (1920 x 1080), 24 fps, one continuous locked-off take. It will be shown very softly behind a web page and scrubbed by the scroll wheel, so it must work as a sequence of beautiful, readable stills. The entire frame is one perfectly flat, uniform PURE WHITE field #FFFFFF, identical in every pixel, no vignette, no gradient, no texture, no noise. Everything drawn is clean line work and small flat marks with perfectly square 90-degree corners (zero corner radius; only dots, rings and the lens are round). Strokes are 2 to 4 px (never thicker than 6 px). Colours allowed: ink #201E1D, darkest grey #2D2B2B, dark grey #605D5D, divider grey #999796, mid grey #BAB6B6, light grey #D7D3D3, paper #F8F4F4, and ONE vivid vermilion red #EC3013 (pale red #FFC4B8 only for highlights); no other hue exists; red covers less than 4% of the frame and marks only what is active. No lighting, no shading, no shadows, no glow, no 3D, no dark backgrounds, no neon.
SOFTNESS: the hero object and the words being read stay sharp and legible. Everything secondary (the other papers of the batch, background cards, extra dots, small details) is slightly soft, a light blur of about 1 to 2 px at 1920 px. Fast-moving pieces carry a short light motion blur along their direction of travel (about 3 to 6 px). Never heavy blur, never blur on the hero text.
PACE: fast and busy, like a machine working through a whole batch of papers, not a single page: first the sharp hero action, then immediately a rapid burst of the same action over many soft copies. Many elements move at once, ease-out cubic, straight paths, no bounce, no particles. The first and last frame are each a complete, balanced composition.
COMPOSITION: weighted to the RIGHT; the left 38% of the frame stays almost empty (a page heading will sit there); at least half of the frame stays empty white; everything inside x 10%-90% and y 6%-94%. Coordinates are percentages (x from the left edge, y from the top edge). Camera tripod-static: no zoom, no pan, no cuts, no fades to black.

WORDS ALLOWED (exact spelling; they come from the project's own synthetic sample report, all values fictional): Riverdale Diagnostics, Synthetic Demo Laboratory, Comprehensive Health Panel, Patient: Demo Persona A, Collection Date: 15 January 2025, Report ID: SYN-2025-0115-A, and the test rows Hemoglobin 13.8 g/dL 12.0 - 15.5; Vitamin D, 25-Hydroxy 18 ng/mL 30 - 100 (small ink tag LOW); Cholesterol, Total 224 mg/dL < 200 (small red tag HIGH); LDL Cholesterol 148 mg/dL < 100 (small red tag HIGH); HDL Cholesterol 44 mg/dL > 40; Fasting Glucose 96 mg/dL 70 - 99; the file tag PDF; one question mark ? only where stated. No other words or numbers anywhere: all other text is drawn as soft, unreadable bars.

SCENE: the Timeline: a batch of reports becomes isometric bars over time. One true isometric room right of centre (about 46% of the frame wide, centre x 66%, y 52%): a rhombus floor with a faint 8 x 8 grid, a back wall and a left wall in 2 px divider-grey outlines; on the back wall a horizontal band filled pale red #FFE0D9 (the reference range). Above the sharp title position (back wall, top) the words "Cholesterol, Total" in small sharp ink type.
BATCH: seven report papers (each 3% wide, soft, slightly blurred, the first one sharp and showing "15 January 2025") wait in a row at the top left of the room.
MOTION: one after another (0.7 s apart, from 0.3 s) each paper flies in a short arc and lands on the floor where it turns into an isometric column rising straight up to its final height (heights 30%, 38%, 34%, 52%, 46%, 60%, 74% of the wall height). Column faces: top mid grey #BAB6B6, front dark grey #605D5D, side darkest grey #2D2B2B; the last, tallest column is red (top #FF9783, front #EC3013, side #AE1800). The first column carries the sharp small label "15 January 2025" on the floor in front of it and the number "224" in small sharp type on its top; the others are unlabelled and soft. Columns are crisp when they stop; while rising they have a short vertical motion blur. At 5.2 s one thin red ring pulses once around the red column's top.
FIRST FRAME: empty room, band, title words, papers waiting. LAST FRAME: seven columns standing, the last red.

AVOID: any words other than the allowed ones; blurred hero text or heavy blur; people; gradients; glow; shadows; rounded corners; solid dark masses; dark or black backgrounds; camera movement or cuts; any colour other than ink, greys and one red.
