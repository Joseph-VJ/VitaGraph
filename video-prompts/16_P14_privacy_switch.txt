A. WHAT THIS CLIP IS FOR
------------------------
VitaGraph is privacy-aware. With the setting On, retrieved passages may be sent to the AI model to write an answer. With the setting Off, nothing leaves the computer: answers are composed locally, quoting the user's own passages, and are labelled "Evidence only". This clip shows the switch being turned Off and the consequence. Use it in a demo film or as an alternative frame set.


B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
POST /api/ai/privacy saves allow_api without any network call and persists it. When allow_api is off (or no key is set), the agent route never starts the AI runtime; it runs the evidence-only path: persona-scoped retrieval from the local vector store and an offline composer that quotes the passages with their citations. The page marks such answers as evidence only. No words are drawn in this video.
