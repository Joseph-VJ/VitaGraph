A. WHAT THIS CLIP IS FOR
------------------------
The standalone OCR tool: a picture of a printed lab slip goes in, and every printed line is found, boxed, read and listed as clean text, with the number of lines, characters and mean confidence summarised above, a toggle that shows or hides the boxes on the original, and a red "needs review" mark on any line the engine is not sure about. Use it in a demo film or as an alternative frame set.


B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
The image is sent to POST /api/tools/ocr. The backend runs RapidOCR on the CPU, in memory, and never stores the image. It returns the text, the mean confidence, and every line with its confidence (0-100) and its bounding box. The page draws the original with the boxes on top (toggle "Show boxes" / "Hide boxes"), lists the lines in the TEXT panel, marks every line under 70% confidence with a small deep red "needs review" label, and offers "Copy text" and "Download .md". No words or numbers are drawn in this video.
