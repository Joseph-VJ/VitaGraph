Clean 3D explainer animation, modern minimal look: light grey background, matte white and charcoal objects, one red accent colour, soft studio light with soft shadows, slow smooth camera drift, bold simple sans-serif title text. The only text allowed is the short title and the one small line for each stage given below. No numbers or digits anywhere, no data, no patient or medical details: all document content is drawn as grey bars. A row of seven small circles at the bottom shows progress (finished = charcoal, current = red). 21 seconds, smooth, instantly easy to understand at a glance.

Seven stages in one continuous shot, 3 seconds each, left to right. Each stage has a big one-word title at the top and one small line under it: 1) Received: PDF uploaded. A document file slides down into an open upload box and settles inside. The lid closes with a soft click and a check mark appears on the box. The first circle at the bottom turns red. 2) Extracted: Text pulled from every page. The file opens into a fan of pages. A thin red scanner light sweeps over each page in turn. On pages with real text, the lines lift off and float onto a clean text sheet on the right. One page is only a picture: red boxes outline its lines, the rough lines turn crisp, and they float onto the same sheet. The sheet fills with neat lines and every page gets a check mark. 3) Chunked: Text split into small chunks. The clean text sheet is sliced by a red line into several small blocks. The blocks swing apart in 3D and float forward. Each block gets a small tag showing which page it came from: a tiny page with a red marker on it. 4) Embedded: Chunks become vectors. Each block slides into a simple machine and comes out as a strip of small coloured cells, like a barcode. That strip is its vector. Many blocks follow, one after another, and a neat row of strips builds up. 5) Indexed: Stored for search, only for you. The strips fly into a tall database cylinder. Inside it, one compartment with a red outline and a closing lock takes all of them; the other compartments stay grey and locked. A search light sweeps over the cylinder and lights up only the strips inside the red compartment. 6) Graphed: Facts and links mapped. From the stored blocks, small dots of different shapes pop out as facts and connect with thin lines into a knowledge graph. The graph floats in 3D and turns slowly. 7) Answer: Answered with real evidence. A speech bubble with a question mark appears. The matching blocks and graph dots light up red. An answer card builds up from grey bars, and small red chips on the card connect with thin red lines back to the lit blocks. All seven circles are finished.
