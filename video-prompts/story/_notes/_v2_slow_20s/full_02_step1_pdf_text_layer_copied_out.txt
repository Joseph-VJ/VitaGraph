STEP 1: "the words are copied out of the PDF". At 0:00 the viewer sees keyframe K0 exactly: the digital lab report page on the left (all text in crisp ink, a small ink tag PDF at its bottom-left corner), an empty dashed output box on the right with one short blinking-style ink caret bar at its top-left corner (static), the progress rail with step 1 red.
0:00.4-0:01.0 the TEXT LAYER separates: a transparent copy of the page (same size, 2 px dashed ink outline, no fill) lifts 3% up and right, carrying the same text in ink exactly aligned, while the text on the page itself fades to faint grey #BBB8B7. This shows that the words live on their own layer inside the file.
0:01.0-0:03.4 COPYING: a thin red horizontal bar (4 px, as wide as the layer) steps down the layer line by line through all 14 text lines (header, subheader, title, three patient lines, table header, six table rows, footer), about 0.17 s per line. As the bar passes a line, every WORD of that line detaches from the layer as a small paper tile (2 px divider outline, the word in ink), glides straight right along a thin dashed path into the output box and lands exactly in its place there, in the same order, so that the output fills line by line with clean plain text; table rows land as aligned columns (Test, Result, Unit, Reference range) and the flags land as plain words (LOW, HIGH). At any moment 10 to 20 tiles are in flight at once. When a tile lands its outline vanishes and it becomes plain text.
0:03.4-0:04.0 the layer fades out; the page stays at 35% opacity and a black square with a white check mark (4% wide) appears in its top-right corner; the rail's first square turns ink and the second square turns red. Hold.

Duration 4 seconds. Hold perfectly still for the first 0.4 s and the last 0.4 s. Everything that exists at the start stays exactly where it is unless this paragraph moves it.
