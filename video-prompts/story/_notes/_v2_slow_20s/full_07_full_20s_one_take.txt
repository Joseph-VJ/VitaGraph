PROMPT (paste this whole block)
==========================================================================================
A 20-second silent flat 2D motion-graphics film in five steps that shows, in the simplest literal way, how a lab-report PDF becomes searchable: (1) the words are copied out of the PDF, (2) a scanned picture is boxed and read, (3) the text is cut into passages that keep their address, (4) each passage becomes a row of numbers and a place on a meaning map, (5) the passages are stored in the user's own locked panel and a search only looks inside it. One continuous locked-off take, square 1:1, 24 fps. Anyone watching with the sound off must be able to say: "The words are copied out of the file, or read from a picture, cut into passages that remember where they came from, turned into numbers, and stored in my own locked panel, where the search only looks."

LOOK: flat 2D vector motion graphics in strict Swiss International (Modernist) style, square 1:1 frame, 24 fps, silent. The entire frame is one perfectly flat, uniform warm light grey #EAE9E9, edge to edge, no vignette, no gradient, no texture, no noise. Every shape is flat single-colour geometry with razor-sharp edges and perfectly square 90-degree corners (zero corner radius; only dots are round). Colours allowed: paper #F8F4F4, light grey #D7D3D3, faint ink #BBB8B7, divider grey #999796, dark grey #605D5D, ink #201E1D, and ONE vivid vermilion red #EC3013 (deep red #AE1800 and pale reds #FFC4B8 / #FFE0D9 only for small marks); no other hue exists. Red marks only the thing that is active right now. No lighting, no shading, no shadows, no glow, no bloom, no blur, no depth of field, no motion blur, no film grain, no reflections, no 3D, no photographic realism, no dark backgrounds, no neon, no holograms. Every frame must look like a crisp finished vector screenshot of a clean software interface. TYPOGRAPHY: all text is set in a clean neo-grotesque sans-serif (Archivo look), regular and extra-bold weights only, ink on paper. The ONLY words and numbers in the film are those of the DOCUMENT TEXT block, spelled exactly, plus the three letters PDF on the file tag and one question mark in step 5.

CAMERA AND SET: tripod-static, orthographic, straight-on; no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no cuts, no fades to black. Coordinates are percentages of the frame (x from the left edge, y from the top edge). Everything stays inside x 4%-96%, y 8%-90%; the bottom-right corner x 74%-100%, y 86%-100% stays empty. The set is always the same: LEFT ZONE x 7-47, y 18-72 holds the source page (portrait, 40% wide, 54% tall); RIGHT ZONE x 53-93, y 18-72 holds the output (a dashed outline box that fills with text, later cards or panels); a PROGRESS RAIL at x 7-70, y 84-87 shows five small squares (36 px at 1600 px) joined by a thin grey line: finished steps are ink, the current step is red, upcoming steps are outlined.

MOTION: rich and dense but precise: many elements move at the same time, with overlapping flights, every element perfectly crisp. Ease-out cubic, straight horizontal or vertical paths, drawn ahead of each flying element as a thin 2 px dashed grey line. No bounce, no overshoot, no particles, no trails other than those dashed paths. Appearing and disappearing elements fade over 0.4 s.

STEP 1: "the words are copied out of the PDF". At 0:00 the viewer sees keyframe K0 exactly: the digital lab report page on the left (all text in crisp ink, a small ink tag PDF at its bottom-left corner), an empty dashed output box on the right with one short blinking-style ink caret bar at its top-left corner (static), the progress rail with step 1 red.
0:00.4-0:01.0 the TEXT LAYER separates: a transparent copy of the page (same size, 2 px dashed ink outline, no fill) lifts 3% up and right, carrying the same text in ink exactly aligned, while the text on the page itself fades to faint grey #BBB8B7. This shows that the words live on their own layer inside the file.
0:01.0-0:03.4 COPYING: a thin red horizontal bar (4 px, as wide as the layer) steps down the layer line by line through all 14 text lines (header, subheader, title, three patient lines, table header, six table rows, footer), about 0.17 s per line. As the bar passes a line, every WORD of that line detaches from the layer as a small paper tile (2 px divider outline, the word in ink), glides straight right along a thin dashed path into the output box and lands exactly in its place there, in the same order, so that the output fills line by line with clean plain text; table rows land as aligned columns (Test, Result, Unit, Reference range) and the flags land as plain words (LOW, HIGH). At any moment 10 to 20 tiles are in flight at once. When a tile lands its outline vanishes and it becomes plain text.
0:03.4-0:04.0 the layer fades out; the page stays at 35% opacity and a black square with a white check mark (4% wide) appears in its top-right corner; the rail's first square turns ink and the second square turns red. Hold.

STEP 2: "a picture of words is read". 0:00-0:00.6 the page of step 1 and the full output fade out (0.4 s) while the SCANNED page fades in (keyframe K1b exactly): the same report, but drawn as a photo of paper: the sheet tilted 3 degrees counter-clockwise, paper in dull grey #E3DFDF, the text rendered rough and slightly blocky (made of visible square pixels, about 0.2% of the frame each), a few tiny grey specks, a faint fold line, and four ink viewfinder corner brackets around it. The output box on the right is empty again. The rail shows step 2 red.
0:00.6-0:01.2 a thin red horizontal scan bar (4 px) sweeps over the scan from top to bottom.
0:01.2-0:02.6 FINDING: in reading order, about 40 thin red rectangles (2 px) draw tightly around every word and every table cell of the scan, very fast, 4 to 6 boxes at a time. Each box gets a tiny ink bar above its top-right corner (0.5% tall; its length shows how sure the reading is, about 90% of the box width). ONE box, around the number 18 in the Vitamin D row, is dashed red and carries a SHORT red bar instead: a weak reading.
0:02.6-0:03.7 READING: box after box (4 to 6 at a time) the rough pixel text inside the box turns crisp (same word, clean type), a paper tile with that word detaches, flies straight right along a thin dashed path and lands in its place in the output box, exactly as in step 1; the red box fades once its word has left. The weak word 18 flies last, and a short red underline (5 px) stays under it in the output.
0:03.7-0:04.0 the scan stays faded to 35% with a black check square in its top-right corner; the output is full; rail: steps 1 and 2 ink, step 3 red. Hold.

STEP 3: "cut into passages, each with its address". 0:00.2-0:01.0 the faded scan page on the left shrinks smoothly to a small page (22% wide, 28% tall) at x 5-27, y 24-52 while the output text stays where it is.
0:01.0-0:02.0 CUTTING: one thin red horizontal knife line (4 px, 4% wider than the output text on each side) sweeps once down the text. At three places (after the line "Report ID: SYN-2025-0115-A", after the Vitamin D row, after the HDL Cholesterol row) it leaves a red dashed line that snaps, and the text splits into FOUR passages. Each passage becomes a paper card (2 px divider outline, 20 px padding, x 52-94) and the cards slide apart vertically to 2.5% gaps: card 1 holds the header, title and three patient lines (y 19-33); card 2 holds the Hemoglobin and Vitamin D rows (y 35.5-43.5); card 3 holds the three cholesterol rows (y 46-56); card 4 holds the Fasting Glucose row and the footer line (y 58.5-67).
0:02.0-0:03.4 ADDRESSES: for each card in turn (0.35 s apart), a small page thumbnail (6% wide, 8.5% tall, paper, 2 px outline) fades in on its left (x 43-49) with a red band across it at the height where that passage sits on the real page (card 1: top, card 2: upper middle, card 3: middle, card 4: lower), and at the same time a thin dashed red line (2 px) draws from the small page at the left to the thumbnail, and a short thick red bar appears on the small page's left edge marking the same span: "this passage came from exactly here".
0:03.4-0:04.0 Hold: rail steps 1-3 ink, step 4 red.

STEP 4: "passages become numbers, numbers become a place on a map". 0:00-0:00.5 the small page, thumbnails and dashed lines fade out. 0:00.2-0:01.0 the four cards shrink smoothly (to about 55%) and stack at the left, x 5-29, y 20-65, with a 2.5% gap. 0:00.6 an ENCODER block fades in in the middle (x 34-43, y 30-60): 5 px ink outline, paper fill, three horizontal bars inside (ink, dark grey, mid grey), a small square lamp (1.6%) above it, ink while idle.
A square MAP fades in on the right (x 52-94, y 16-64): paper fill, 3 px divider outline, a faint 2 px light-grey grid of 7 x 8 cells.
0:01.0-0:03.0 EMBEDDING: card by card (0.45 s apart, from card 1 to card 4): the card slides right into the encoder's left face (0.2 s), the lamp turns red (0.25 s), and from the encoder's right face a horizontal STRIP of 24 small squares (each 1.2% wide, touching, in a different pattern of ink, dark grey, mid grey, light grey and a few red, light red and pale red squares: no two strips alike) shoots out to the right (0.3 s) and slides to the map. On the map the strip collapses into ONE ink dot (2.5% wide, with a paper ring) at its place: card 1 lands at (24%, 22%) of the map, card 2 at (74%, 26%), card 3 at (34%, 70%), card 4 at (56%, 76%). Each processed card dims to 50% opacity. The lamp returns to ink after the last card.
0:03.0-0:03.7 MEANING: when the fourth dot lands next to the third, a red line (5 px) joins those two dots and a dashed red ellipse (3 px) draws around them: "passages that mean something similar sit close together". The other two dots stay alone.
0:03.7-0:04.0 Hold: rail steps 1-4 ink, step 5 red.

STEP 5: "stored in my own panel; the search only looks there". 0:00-0:00.4 the dimmed cards, the encoder, the red link, the ellipse and the map grid fade out; the four ink dots stay where they are for a moment.
0:00.4-0:01.0 THREE PANELS fade in side by side, each 26% wide and 48% tall (y 22-70): panel B at x 8-34 and panel C at x 37-63, both with a dashed 3 px grey outline, a mid-grey header square with two grey bars, and their own mid-grey dots (panel B six dots, panel C five dots): other people's data; and MY panel A at x 68-94 with a solid 5 px RED outline, a red header square with an ink bar and a dark grey bar, empty inside.
0:01.0-0:02.2 STORING: the four ink dots move straight into panel A (staggered 0.2 s, ease-out) and settle in a 3 x 2 grid; as each dot lands, a small red square (1% wide) pops onto its upper-right corner: the owner mark.
0:02.2-0:02.6 LOCK: a padlock (ink, square body 4.2% wide, square-cornered U-shaped shackle) above panel A's top-right corner closes (the shackle drops 1.4% into the body) and one thin red ring expands once around it and fades.
0:02.6-0:03.8 SEARCH: a square speech bubble with a single question mark ? fades in at the top left (x 8-17, y 7.5-14). A red line (5 px) draws from it down 1.5%, then right along y 16.5 and down into panel A (0.5 s). Two thin dashed grey branches (3 px) leave the red line above panel B and above panel C, run down toward those panels and are stopped by short thick ink bars (4.3% wide, 0.9% tall) just above them: they never enter. When the red line reaches panel A the four dots flash red one after another (0.12 s apart) and return to ink.
0:03.8-0:04.0 Hold: all five rail squares ink.
