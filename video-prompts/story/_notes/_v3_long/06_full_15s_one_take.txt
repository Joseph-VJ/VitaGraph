A 15-second silent flat 2D motion-graphics film in five fast steps that shows, in the simplest literal way, how a whole batch of lab-report papers becomes searchable: (1) many PDF pages are scanned one after another and the words are copied out of each, (2) a pile of scanned pictures is swept, every word is boxed and read, (3) all the text is cut into passages that keep their address on the page, (4) every passage becomes a row of numbers and a dot on a meaning map where similar passages sit close, (5) all passages are stored in the user's own locked panel and a search only looks inside it. One continuous locked-off take, square 1:1, 24 fps. Anyone watching with the sound off must be able to say: "Many papers are scanned, their words are read and cut into passages that remember where they came from, turned into numbers, and stored in my own locked panel, where the search only looks."

LOOK: flat 2D vector motion graphics in strict Swiss International (Modernist) style, square 1:1 frame, 24 fps, silent. The entire frame is one perfectly flat, uniform warm light grey #EAE9E9, edge to edge, no vignette, no gradient, no texture, no noise. Every shape is flat single-colour geometry with perfectly square 90-degree corners (zero corner radius; only dots are round). Colours allowed: paper #F8F4F4, light grey #D7D3D3, faint ink #BBB8B7, divider grey #999796, dark grey #605D5D, ink #201E1D, and ONE vivid vermilion red #EC3013 (deep red #AE1800 and pale reds #FFC4B8 / #FFE0D9 only for small marks); no other hue exists. Red marks only the thing that is active right now. No lighting, no shading, no shadows, no glow, no bloom, no film grain, no 3D, no photographic realism, no dark backgrounds, no neon, no holograms. It must look like a crisp, finished, modern software interface.
SOFTNESS (important): the hero page and the words being read stay sharp and fully legible at all times. Everything secondary is slightly soft, like a very shallow depth of field: the other pages of the stack, the cards and thumbnails behind the hero cards, the extra dots, and small background details carry a light blur of about 1 to 2 px at 1600 px. Fast-moving word tiles, strips and dots carry a short, light motion blur along their direction of travel (about 3 to 6 px). Never heavy blur, never blur on the hero text.
TYPOGRAPHY: all text is set in a clean neo-grotesque sans-serif (Archivo look), regular and extra-bold weights only. The ONLY readable words and numbers in the film are those of the DOCUMENT TEXT block, spelled exactly, plus the three letters PDF on the file tag and one question mark in step 5; text on the soft background pages is simply the same text, too soft to read.

CAMERA AND SET: tripod-static, orthographic, straight-on; no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no cuts, no fades to black. Coordinates are percentages of the frame (x from the left edge, y from the top edge). Everything stays inside x 4%-96%, y 8%-90%; the bottom-right corner x 74%-100%, y 86%-100% stays empty. The set is always the same: LEFT ZONE x 7-47, y 18-72 holds the source: ONE sharp hero page (portrait, 40% wide, 54% tall) on top of a STACK of about five more pages offset up and to the right by 1% steps; RIGHT ZONE x 53-93, y 18-72 holds the output (a dashed outline box that fills with text, later cards, a map, or panels); a PROGRESS RAIL at x 7-70, y 84-87 shows five small squares joined by a thin grey line: finished steps are ink, the current step is red, upcoming steps are outlined.

PACE AND MOTION: fast and busy, like a machine working through a whole batch of papers, not one page. Every step shows the hero page first and then, immediately, a rapid burst of the same action over the rest of the stack. Many elements move at the same time with overlapping flights. Ease-out cubic, straight horizontal or vertical paths, each flying element preceded by a thin 2 px dashed grey path. No bounce, no overshoot, no particles. Appearing and disappearing elements fade over 0.25 s.

DOCUMENT TEXT (the ONLY words and numbers allowed in the film; spell them exactly; they are the project's own synthetic sample report, all values fictional)
  Header (extra-bold):  Riverdale Diagnostics          under it (regular, dark grey):  Synthetic Demo Laboratory
  Title (extra-bold):   Comprehensive Health Panel
  Three lines:          Patient: Demo Persona A
                        Collection Date: 15 January 2025
                        Report ID: SYN-2025-0115-A
  Table header (extra-bold):  Test | Result | Unit | Reference range
  Table rows:           Hemoglobin | 13.8 | g/dL | 12.0 - 15.5
                        Vitamin D, 25-Hydroxy | 18 | ng/mL | 30 - 100 | small ink tag LOW
                        Cholesterol, Total | 224 | mg/dL | < 200 | small red tag HIGH
                        LDL Cholesterol | 148 | mg/dL | < 100 | small red tag HIGH
                        HDL Cholesterol | 44 | mg/dL | > 40
                        Fasting Glucose | 96 | mg/dL | 70 - 99
  Footer line (small):  Interpretation of these values is reserved for the treating clinician.
  File tag on the digital page (white letters on an ink tag):  PDF
  Step 5 only: one question mark ? in a speech-bubble square.
  Nothing else: no captions, no titles, no UI labels, no step names, no logos.
  The ready-made pixel-exact versions of all this are in the keyframes/ folder (see 08_keyframes_how_to_use.txt).

Timecodes below are for the whole film: each step lasts 3 s and starts at 0:00, 0:03, 0:06, 0:09 and 0:12; add that offset to the step's own timecodes. Give the generator keyframes/K0.png as the first frame.

STEP 1 (0:00-0:03): "the words are copied out of every PDF page". At 0:00 the viewer sees keyframe K0: the sharp hero lab report page with the PDF tag, a stack of five more pages behind it (soft), and the empty dashed output box on the right; rail step 1 red.
0:00.25-0:00.65 the hero page's TEXT LAYER lifts 3% up and right (transparent copy, 2 px dashed ink outline, same text in ink, exactly aligned) while the text on the page fades to faint grey.
0:00.65-1.65 COPYING THE HERO PAGE: a thin red bar (4 px) steps down the layer through all 14 text lines in 1.0 s (about 0.07 s per line). As it passes, every word detaches as a small paper tile (2 px outline, word in ink) and streams right along dashed paths into the output box, 20 to 40 tiles in flight at once with a short light motion blur, landing in order so the output fills with clean sharp text; table rows land as aligned columns.
0:01.65-0:02.65 THE BATCH: the finished hero page gets a black check square in its corner, slides left 6% and fades to 35% while the next page of the stack steps forward; then, 0.25 s apart, the next four pages each lift their text layer and burst their words in dense soft-blurred streams into the output box. Behind the sharp hero text, extra lines (lighter, soft) stream in from below and scroll upward and out of the top edge, so the box always looks full and busy. Each processed page keeps a small soft check square and slides under the stack.
0:02.65-0:03.0 the extra scrolling lines fade out so that only the sharp clean hero text remains: the last frame equals keyframe K1 (hero page 35% with check square, stack of faded checked pages behind it, output full, rail step 1 ink and step 2 red). Hold.

STEP 2 (0:00-0:03): "a pile of scanned pictures is read". 0:00-0:00.35 the faded digital stack and the output fade out while a PILE of six SCANNED sheets fades in (keyframe K1b): the hero scan on top, sharp, tilted 3 degrees counter-clockwise, dull grey paper #E3DFDF, rough blocky text made of visible square pixels, specks, a faint fold line, four ink viewfinder corner brackets; five more scanned sheets under it, each tilted a different 1 to 4 degrees and soft. The output box is empty again; rail step 2 red.
0:00.35-0:00.7 a thin red scan bar (4 px) sweeps over the hero scan from top to bottom.
0:00.7-1.4 FINDING: about 40 thin red rectangles (2 px) draw around every word and table cell of the hero scan, 8 to 10 at a time, each with a tiny ink confidence bar above its top-right corner; ONE box, around the number 18 in the Vitamin D row, is dashed red with a SHORT red bar (a weak reading).
0:01.4-0:02.0 READING: the rough pixel text inside each box turns crisp and a word tile flies straight right along a dashed path into the output box, 30 or more tiles in flight at once with short light motion blur; the red boxes fade as their words leave; 18 flies last and keeps a short red underline in the output.
0:02.0-0:02.75 THE PILE: the scan bar sweeps over the next five sheets in quick succession (0.15 s each); on each sheet about 20 soft red boxes flash for 0.1 s, then the sheet gets a soft check square and slides under the pile. Behind the sharp output text, lighter soft lines stream in and scroll upward and out of the top edge.
0:02.75-0:03.0 the scrolling lines fade out; the last frame equals keyframe K2 (scan faded to 35% with a black check square, pile behind it, output full, red underline under 18, rail steps 1-2 ink, step 3 red). Hold.

STEP 3 (0:00-0:03): "cut into passages, each with its address". 0:00.15-0:00.7 the faded scan and its pile shrink smoothly into a small stack of pages (22% wide, 28% tall) at x 5-27, y 24-52: one small sharp hero page on top of three soft pages behind it; the output text stays where it is.
0:00.7-1.3 CUTTING: one thin red knife line (4 px, 4% wider than the text on each side) sweeps once down the output text (0.35 s) and leaves three red dashed cuts (after the line "Report ID: SYN-2025-0115-A", after the Vitamin D row, after the HDL Cholesterol row). The text splits into FOUR paper cards (2 px divider outline, x 52-94) that slide apart vertically to 2.5% gaps (0.25 s): card 1 holds the header, title and three patient lines (y 19-33); card 2 the Hemoglobin and Vitamin D rows (y 35.5-43.5); card 3 the three cholesterol rows (y 46-56); card 4 the Fasting Glucose row and the footer line (y 58.5-67). At once, behind every sharp hero card, a deck of two soft blurred cards appears, offset 1% down and right: the same cuts happen on every other page.
0:01.3-0:02.3 ADDRESSES: card by card (0.12 s apart) a small page thumbnail (6% wide, 8.5% tall, 2 px outline) fades in at x 43-49 with a red band across it at the height where that passage sits on the real page, a dashed red line (2 px) draws from the small page to it, and a short thick red bar marks the span on the small page's left edge. In a quick burst (0.4 s) about 20 soft small thumbnails with dashed red lines flash in for the deck cards, then fade out by 0:02.7: every passage of every page gets an address.
0:02.3-0:03.0 the last frame equals keyframe K3 (small page stack with red bars, four sharp cards with thumbnails and dashed red lines, soft decks behind the cards, rail steps 1-3 ink, step 4 red). Hold.

STEP 4 (0:00-0:03): "passages become numbers, numbers become a place on a map". 0:00-0:00.3 the small page stack, thumbnails and dashed lines fade out; 0:00.1-0:00.6 the four hero cards shrink to about 55% and stack at the left (x 5-29, y 20-65) together with their soft decks; 0:00.3-0:00.6 an ENCODER block fades in in the middle (x 34-43, y 30-60: 5 px ink outline, paper fill, three horizontal bars inside in ink, dark grey and mid grey, a small square lamp above it, ink while idle) and a square MAP fades in on the right (x 52-94, y 16-64: paper fill, 3 px divider outline, faint 7 x 8 grid).
0:00.6-1.3 THE HERO CARDS: the four hero cards slide into the encoder 0.15 s apart; the lamp turns red; four STRIPS of 24 small squares (each 1.2% wide, touching, a different mix of ink, dark grey, mid grey, light grey and a few red, light red and pale red squares) shoot out to the right with a short light motion blur and each collapses into ONE sharp ink dot (2.5% wide, with a paper ring) on the map: card 1 at (24%, 22%) of the map, card 2 at (74%, 26%), card 3 at (34%, 70%), card 4 at (56%, 76%).
0:01.3-2.3 THE BATCH: about 28 more small soft cards pour from the left into the encoder in a rapid stream (0.03 s apart); 28 strips fly out and each collapses into a SOFT mid-grey dot (about 1.6% wide) on the map, forming four loose clusters around the map; the lamp stays red during the burst and returns to ink at 0:02.3.
0:02.3-0:02.8 MEANING: a red line (5 px) joins the two nearest hero dots (cards 3 and 4) and a dashed red ellipse (3 px) draws around them: passages that mean something similar sit close together.
0:02.8-0:03.0 the last frame equals keyframe K4 (dimmed cards at the left, encoder, map with the four sharp dots and about 30 soft grey dots, red link and ellipse, rail steps 1-4 ink, step 5 red). Hold.

STEP 5 (0:00-0:03): "stored in my own panel; the search only looks there". 0:00-0:00.3 the dimmed cards, the encoder, the red link, the ellipse and the map grid fade out; the dots stay for a moment.
0:00.2-0:00.6 THREE PANELS fade in side by side, each 26% wide and 48% tall (y 22-70): panel B at x 8-34 and panel C at x 37-63 with a dashed 3 px grey outline, a mid-grey header square with two grey bars, and their own mid-grey dots (panel B 18 dots, panel C 14 dots in a grid): other people's data; and MY panel A at x 68-94 with a solid 5 px RED outline, a red header square with an ink bar and a dark grey bar, empty inside.
0:00.6-1.4 STORING: all 34 dots (the four sharp hero dots and the 30 soft grey ones, which turn ink as they move) stream from the map into panel A in a fast cascade (0.02 s apart, short light motion blur) and settle in a grid of six columns; as each lands a small red square (1% wide) pops onto its upper-right corner: the owner mark.
0:01.4-0:01.7 LOCK: a padlock (ink, square body 4.2% wide, square-cornered U-shaped shackle) above panel A's top-right corner closes (the shackle drops 1.4% into the body) and one thin red ring expands once around it and fades.
0:01.7-0:02.8 SEARCH: a square speech bubble with a single question mark ? fades in at the top left (x 8-17, y 7.5-14). A red line (5 px) draws from it down 1.5%, then right along y 16.5 and down into panel A (0.4 s). Two thin dashed grey branches (3 px) leave the red line above panel B and above panel C and run down toward those panels, stopped by short thick ink bars (4.3% wide, 0.9% tall) just above them: they never enter. When the red line reaches panel A, a red wave sweeps across the dots in A row by row (0.5 s) and each dot returns to ink.
0:02.8-0:03.0 the last frame equals keyframe K5 (two grey panels, my red panel with 34 ink dots with red owner squares, closed padlock, ? bubble, red line only to my panel, blocked branches, rail all five ink). Hold.

AVOID: misspelled, garbled or extra text; blurred hero text or heavy blur; captions or labels; dark or black backgrounds; neon, glow or bloom; shadows; gradients; rounded corners; people or hands; camera movement, zoom, pan or cuts; 3D or photographic realism.
