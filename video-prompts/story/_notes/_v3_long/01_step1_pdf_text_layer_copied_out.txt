A 3-second silent flat 2D motion-graphics clip, part 1 of a 5-part, 15-second film that shows, in the simplest literal way and at a fast, busy pace, how a whole batch of lab-report papers becomes searchable. One continuous locked-off take, square 1:1, 24 fps. Anyone watching with the sound off must be able to say: "Many PDF pages are scanned one after another, and the words inside each page are copied out in a flash."

LOOK: flat 2D vector motion graphics in strict Swiss International (Modernist) style, square 1:1 frame, 24 fps, silent. The entire frame is one perfectly flat, uniform warm light grey #EAE9E9, edge to edge, no vignette, no gradient, no texture, no noise. Every shape is flat single-colour geometry with perfectly square 90-degree corners (zero corner radius; only dots are round). Colours allowed: paper #F8F4F4, light grey #D7D3D3, faint ink #BBB8B7, divider grey #999796, dark grey #605D5D, ink #201E1D, and ONE vivid vermilion red #EC3013 (deep red #AE1800 and pale reds #FFC4B8 / #FFE0D9 only for small marks); no other hue exists. Red marks only the thing that is active right now. No lighting, no shading, no shadows, no glow, no bloom, no film grain, no 3D, no photographic realism, no dark backgrounds, no neon, no holograms. It must look like a crisp, finished, modern software interface.
SOFTNESS (important): the hero page and the words being read stay sharp and fully legible at all times. Everything secondary is slightly soft, like a very shallow depth of field: the other pages of the stack, the cards and thumbnails behind the hero cards, the extra dots, and small background details carry a light blur of about 1 to 2 px at 1600 px. Fast-moving word tiles, strips and dots carry a short, light motion blur along their direction of travel (about 3 to 6 px). Never heavy blur, never blur on the hero text.
TYPOGRAPHY: all text is set in a clean neo-grotesque sans-serif (Archivo look), regular and extra-bold weights only. The ONLY readable words and numbers in the film are those of the DOCUMENT TEXT block, spelled exactly, plus the three letters PDF on the file tag and one question mark in step 5; text on the soft background pages is simply the same text, too soft to read.

CAMERA AND SET: tripod-static, orthographic, straight-on; no zoom, no pan, no tilt, no cuts, no fades to black. Coordinates are percentages of the frame (x from the left edge, y from the top edge). Everything stays inside x 4%-96%, y 8%-90%; the bottom-right corner x 74%-100%, y 86%-100% stays empty. The set is always the same: LEFT ZONE x 7-47, y 18-72 holds the source: ONE sharp hero page (portrait, 40% wide, 54% tall) on top of a STACK of about five more pages offset up and to the right by 1% steps; RIGHT ZONE x 53-93, y 18-72 holds the output (a dashed outline box that fills with text, later cards, a map, or panels); a PROGRESS RAIL at x 7-70, y 84-87 shows five small squares joined by a thin grey line: finished steps are ink, the current step is red, upcoming steps are outlined.

PACE AND MOTION: fast and busy, like a machine working through a whole batch of papers, not one page. Every step shows the hero page first and then, immediately, a rapid burst of the same action over the rest of the stack. Many elements move at the same time with overlapping flights. Ease-out cubic, straight horizontal or vertical paths, each flying element preceded by a thin 2 px dashed grey path. No bounce, no overshoot, no particles. Appearing and disappearing elements fade over 0.25 s.

STEP 1 (0:00-0:03): "the words are copied out of every PDF page". At 0:00 the viewer sees keyframe K0: the sharp hero lab report page with the PDF tag, a stack of five more pages behind it (soft), and the empty dashed output box on the right; rail step 1 red.
0:00.25-0:00.65 the hero page's TEXT LAYER lifts 3% up and right (transparent copy, 2 px dashed ink outline, same text in ink, exactly aligned) while the text on the page fades to faint grey.
0:00.65-1.65 COPYING THE HERO PAGE: a thin red bar (4 px) steps down the layer through all 14 text lines in 1.0 s (about 0.07 s per line). As it passes, every word detaches as a small paper tile (2 px outline, word in ink) and streams right along dashed paths into the output box, 20 to 40 tiles in flight at once with a short light motion blur, landing in order so the output fills with clean sharp text; table rows land as aligned columns.
0:01.65-0:02.65 THE BATCH: the finished hero page gets a black check square in its corner, slides left 6% and fades to 35% while the next page of the stack steps forward; then, 0.25 s apart, the next four pages each lift their text layer and burst their words in dense soft-blurred streams into the output box. Behind the sharp hero text, extra lines (lighter, soft) stream in from below and scroll upward and out of the top edge, so the box always looks full and busy. Each processed page keeps a small soft check square and slides under the stack.
0:02.65-0:03.0 the extra scrolling lines fade out so that only the sharp clean hero text remains: the last frame equals keyframe K1 (hero page 35% with check square, stack of faded checked pages behind it, output full, rail step 1 ink and step 2 red). Hold.

Duration 3 seconds. Hold perfectly still for the first 0.25 s and the last 0.25 s. Everything that exists at the start stays exactly where it is unless this paragraph moves it.

AVOID: misspelled, garbled or extra text; blurred hero text or heavy blur; captions or labels; dark or black backgrounds; neon, glow or bloom; shadows; gradients; rounded corners; people or hands; camera movement, zoom, pan or cuts; 3D or photographic realism.
