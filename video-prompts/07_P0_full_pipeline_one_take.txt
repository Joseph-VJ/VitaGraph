A. WHAT THIS CLIP IS FOR
------------------------
The whole Upload page interactive stage in one shot: a report is parsed, its scanned page is read by OCR, its text is cut into passages that keep page and character spans, every passage becomes 384 numbers, and the vectors are stored only in this user's own scope, where a query finds the five closest passages. Scrubbing the stage from left to right walks through these five steps, matching the five rows the page lists on the left (01 Parse digital text, 02 Read scanned pages (OCR), 03 Chunk with provenance, 04 Embed passages, 05 Index, scoped to user).
