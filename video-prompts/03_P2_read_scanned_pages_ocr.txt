B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
Pages without a text layer are rendered to an image and read by RapidOCR on the CPU, locally, in memory. OCR returns each text line with a bounding box and a confidence between 0 and 1. Lines with low confidence are marked "needs review" in the app; they are never silently trusted. In the reference demo the scanned page has eight lines with confidences 0.97, 0.96, 0.95, 0.97, 0.93, 0.68, 0.94, 0.99; the sixth line (0.68) needs review. Those proportions are used below; no number is ever drawn.
