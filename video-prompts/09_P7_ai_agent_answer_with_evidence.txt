A. WHAT THIS CLIP IS FOR
------------------------
The heart of VitaGraph: a question about the user's own reports is answered by the AI Agent, which calls its four read-only tools one after another, retrieves evidence passages from the user's reports with the exact cited characters highlighted, and writes a grounded answer in which every statement carries a numbered citation chip that points back to its passage. Use it in a demo film or as an alternative frame set.


B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
The browser sends the conversation to POST /api/agent/stream and receives Server-Sent Events: status, step, thinking, tool_call, tool_result, text_delta, stats, completed. Before any model call, safety gates run (clinical-boundary refusal, prompt-injection sanitising). The agent can see exactly four read-only tools, all locked to the user's own persona by the backend: list_reports, search_reports (vector search returning evidence cards with report, page and character offsets), get_measurements, graph_lookup. The page shows a trajectory panel: one row per step with a 12 px square marker (red filled while running, ink filled when done, hollow deep red if failed), a stats line (time, steps, tool calls, tokens), then the answer streamed as Markdown. Citation chips [n] (deep red) open the passage slip: the stored page text with the cited characters marked in pale red (#FFE0D9). Question cards have a 2 px ink top rule and a small deep-red "Q1" label. No words or numbers are drawn in this video.
