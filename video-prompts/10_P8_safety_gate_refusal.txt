A. WHAT THIS CLIP IS FOR
------------------------
VitaGraph is an educational evidence tool, not a diagnostic service. When a question asks for a diagnosis, a treatment or a prescription, the safety gate stops it BEFORE the AI model is ever called (fail-closed). This clip shows exactly that: a question card travels toward the agent, meets the gate, the gate closes red, the agent's area on the other side stays completely dormant (no tool runs, nothing is sent), and a refusal card unfolds in the app's own "Declined by policy" style. Use it in a demo film or as an alternative frame set.


B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
In stream_agent, the question is classified first. If it needs a boundary response, the backend immediately streams the fixed boundary text and a completed event with status "refused" and ai_status "not_used": no runtime is started, no tool is called, no passage is sent anywhere. Then the turn is saved. A second gate removes prompt-injection text; a message that is only injection is refused the same way. On the page the refusal is a card with a pale red fill (#FFF2EF), a red top rule and a small pale red tag ("Declined by policy"). No words are drawn in this video.
