A. WHAT THIS CLIP IS FOR
------------------------
The standalone Text to Graph tool: any pasted text is read in the browser, its entities (names, dates, values, terms) are found and marked in the text, and they become a small rotating knowledge graph linked to the document and to the sentences they appear in. Use it in a demo film or as an alternative frame set.


B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
Pure client-side extraction, no AI, deterministic: the text is split into sentences; entities are found (values with units, dates, names, medical terms); the graph has one Document node (filled square), sentence nodes (diamonds), date nodes (rings), value nodes (small grey dots) and name/term nodes (filled circles); every entity links to the sentence it appears in, every sentence links to the Document, so the graph is always connected (at most 60 nodes). It is drawn with the same software-3D canvas as the Knowledge Graph (floor grid, gentle curved edges, slow rotation). A table lists every entity with its value and its character span in the text. No words or numbers are drawn in this video.

All objects stay between 10% and 90% of the frame on both axes; the bottom-right corner region (right of 74% and below 86%) stays empty field.

The result table writes itself: heading bars, the ink rule drawing left to right, then six rows appearing one every 0.15 s with their bars and rules.

6.6 s to 8.0 s  The graph keeps turning slowly; everything else stays still. The clip ends mid-rotation, perfectly sharp.
