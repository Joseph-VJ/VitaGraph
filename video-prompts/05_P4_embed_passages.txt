B. WHAT THE REAL PROCESS DOES (so the picture is truthful)
----------------------------------------------------------
VitaGraph runs a local sentence-embedding model on every chunk. Each chunk becomes a vector of 384 floating-point numbers (positive and negative values). Similar passages get similar vectors, which is how the vector search later finds evidence. Nothing leaves the computer in this step. In the picture, a cell's colour shows the sign of a value (red = positive, grey = negative) and its strength shows the size of the value. The 48 x 8 strip has exactly 384 cells. The matrix has one row per chunk (24 rows) and 96 columns (each column summarising four values). No number is ever drawn.
